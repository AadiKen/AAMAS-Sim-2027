# OpenFOAM 11 steady-yaw rotating region

Steady yaw uses the OpenFOAM Foundation 11 `solidBody` mesh mover on a `rotating` cell zone. A closed, 48-sided cylindrical STL creates the zone and its `rotating` face zone. `createBaffles`, `splitBaffles`, and `createNonConformalCouples` form the sliding interface. The resulting non-conformal cyclic patches are `nonConformalCyclic_on_nonCouple1` and `nonConformalCyclic_on_nonCouple2`. This follows the Foundation 11 `incompressibleVoF/mixerVessel` pattern; Foundation 11 uses non-conformal coupling (NCC) for this rotating interface rather than an older AMI dictionary.

The cylinder is centered at the case reference point. Its radius is the largest hull vertex distance from the yaw axis plus two-thirds of a base cell; its caps are one base cell beyond the hull's vertical extrema. Generation rejects a cylinder that does not fit the stationary domain. `snappyHexMesh` puts the hull inside the rigid zone. The mover rotates that zone about Foam `(0, 0, -1)`, matching positive FRD yaw in `bcod-openfoam-frd-v1`; angular rate in rad/s is converted to rpm. The outer zone stays stationary. The generated case records the actual center, radius, caps, patches, and rate in `case_metadata.json`.

For the tiny qualification box, the center was `(0, 0, 0)` m, cylinder radius `0.3692582404` m, and caps `z = ±0.25` m. The base box fixture had 728 cells; interface refinement and baffle creation gave the completed NCC mesh 1,624 cells. At 60 rpm, the mesh-only run reached 720° and the laminar VOF run reached 360° (1 s). The measured hull point error was below `5e-15` m at every quarter-turn checkpoint. Mesh-only minimum volume, maximum non-orthogonality, and maximum skewness stayed `0.000146252231 m³`, `31.87527459°`, and `1.966526528` at 0°, 90°, 180°, 270°, 360°, and 720°. There were no negative-volume cells or low-quality face tets. The initial coarse mesh had 28 small-determinant and 96 concave cells, unchanged at every checkpoint; `checkMesh` therefore reported two existing quality findings.

The production-generated tiny case completed one fluid revolution with 261 finite force samples, maximum Co `0.5028248344`, maximum alphaCo `0.3154028569`, and minimum NCC average coverage `0.998798441`. Its initial and final mesh quality were identical: minimum volume `0.0001451774269 m³`, maximum non-orthogonality `31.88455013°`, and maximum skewness `1.962315743`. The alpha range was `-3.026871206e-5` to `1.000001657`. These values establish execution and mesh stability for the tiny case, not hydrodynamic accuracy or coefficient convergence.

Generation and execution fail if the zone/interface does not initialize, the hull does not move with the commanded sign and center, the mesh degrades or inverts, NCC coverage falls below `0.995`, or the solver emits non-finite values. Forced small-amplitude translation and rotation retain their displacement-based mover. Steady roll and pitch are not routed through this yaw cylinder: their rotating zones and free-surface treatment need separate design and qualification. This qualification used serial laminar VOF; turbulent and parallel steady-yaw execution remain unqualified.

Raw logs and the machine-readable summary are in `stage3_results/stage3a-rotation/` in the working tree. That results directory is ignored by Git.
